kaomoji data: sources and notices

Faces (strings) were collected from these sources. Scores, tags, phrases and
picks were produced by this project unless a source is named for them.

- kmoji: faces; tags (redistribution of tags allowed).
- kaomojiru: faces; tags (redistribution of tags allowed).
- gsozai: faces. Its tags feed derived data only; the category tree is not
  mirrored.
- fontvibe kaomoji dataset: faces and tags, CC BY 4.0 with an MIT notice;
  its "kaosute" pool is excluded from redistributed builds.
- emojidb: faces; per-query copy counts used only as a derived popularity
  prior (the lists themselves are not published).
- emojicombos: faces; single-word user tags.
- kaomojikuma: faces; headings used for vectors only.
- Japanese IME dictionaries: faces; readings used in reduced form only,
  never a whole dictionary.
- kaomojisjp: face strings (CC0); no labels or ordering from it are used.
- ekohrt/emoticon_kaomoji_dataset: faces; its tags are not shipped.

This build's licence policy is recorded in the manifest (MANI `policy`):
a `private` build may contain tags from sources marked "limited" above and
must not be redistributed; public builds are filtered by the public policy
(docs/public-data.md).
